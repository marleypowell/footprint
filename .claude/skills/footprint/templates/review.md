# Performance review

A self-assessment for one review period. Window: the period (ask; save it in `profile.md`). Inputs:
the window's overview and `facts.json`, the notes of its items, `profile.md`, `framework.md`.

1. **Summary.** Three to five sentences: the period's main threads of work and the user's part in
   them.
2. **Highlights.** The three to six biggest initiatives and features by the window's active days
   (the overview's tables). For each: what it was, the user's part, the outcome, its citations, and
   the competency in `framework.md` it shows.
3. **By competency.** Each competency in `framework.md`, with the evidence for it from the notes, or
   "nothing in the data" for the user to fill in.
4. **The rest.** Smaller work in aggregate (fixes, the work naming no issue), docs written,
   AI-assisted work (`agent_commits`).
5. **Numbers.** A short table of the window's totals, each with its facts.json name.
6. **Gaps and next.** What the data cannot show (the coverage section), and the open questions.

Lead with outcomes over activity, and the user's part over the team's.
